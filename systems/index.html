Proprietary Algorithmic Trading & AI Agent Infrastructure

Personal project — built, maintained, and operated solo · 2024–present · macOS, Python, Node.js, SQLite, launchd

Core Infrastructure
  • Engineered trade-core, a shared 15-minute bar store and signal ledger backed by SQLite, ingesting equity bars from Alpaca IEX (RTH) with Yahoo Finance as an extended-hours and crypto fallback; all 10+ downstream agents read from a single authoritative store.
  • Built a statistical evidence-gating system using Wilson lower-bound confidence intervals (≥30 signals, ≥10 days minimum) to determine which signal kinds are PROVEN before they generate live email alerts — false-positive rate reduced to near zero from naïve thresholds.
  • Implemented regime detection and grading pipelines that evaluate every signal outcome net of estimated transaction costs, feeding results back into the ledger automatically after each trade settles.
Signal Agents
  • Headless Flip Watcher — 75-symbol (equities + CME micros futures) signal agent running without TradingView; SuperTrend 3/10 on 15m bars, early rally detection (bull FVG + ≥2.5× volume confirmation), and 1h futures FVG alerts; polls every 2 minutes, live since Sep 2026.
  • Market IV Agent — scans 11 healthcare and S&P sector profiles at 09:45 and 16:30 each trading day for 2–5 bull call spread candidates; grades each spread at expiry through trade-core; consolidates into a single mid-day email digest.
  • Crypto Scanner — monitors 102 coins (Robinhood + Coinbase + Wallet) with stock-like signal logic and a daily bias board; only PROVEN flip kinds email live.
  • Coin Launch Agent — detects newly listed Coinbase/Robinhood coins; applies a plus50 filter (+50% in 1h with ≥40% held-out confirmation gate) and a kill switch; graded in trade-core.
  • Kalshi BTC Caller — read-only agent that calls KXBTC15M prediction market contracts when the gap to strike is large relative to remaining volatility; independently accurate but priced-in at market (break-even after fees — documented as such).
Research & Simulation
  • Paper Lab — isolated, broker-free rules engine for BTC/ETH and 4 CME micro-futures (MNQ/MES/MGC/MCL) with 4 rule archetypes (hold, trend, breakout, reversion); versioned rule sets with paper_start timestamps; generates weekly P&L reports per instrument and rule.
  • Market Lab — irreplaceable research database capturing Kalshi 15m prediction market quotes and CME futures bars; hosts Paper Lab, Event Desk, Sector Research, and the Agent Watchdog under one repository.
  • Sector Breadth Research — weekly Saturday email analyzing 11 sector ETFs + 16 industry groups vs. SPY: green/red day share, average moves, p-value vs. 50% null, and rank vs. prior week — stateless, runs fully in memory.
  • Flux Lab — proprietary FluxCharts-style Smart Money Concepts Pine Script indicator for TradingView; occupies the live chart scanner slot.
Orchestration & Delivery
  • Event Desk — consolidates all agent output into a single 08:55 morning brief (watchlist + BUY/SELL regime grid + evidence) and a ~11:10 market digest (IV scan results for 10 sectors + any ACT signals); drives Robinhood spread ticket execution decisions.
  • Agent Watchdog — runs every 15 minutes via launchd; detects missed scans (compares actual vs. expected run times against NYSE calendar), retries failed email sends from the Gmail outbox, runs flip healthcheck --repair, and verifies Zillow freshness.
  • Zillow/Redfin Scanner — daily Atlanta real estate digest across 51 ZIP codes and 7 markets; uses Redfin polygon/CSV search (Zillow blocked by PerimeterX) with an adaptive quadtree for dense bounding boxes.
  • Email UI — shared multipart HTML/text email renderer (email-ui.js + email_ui.py) used by all 10+ agents; consistent subject-line convention, section hierarchy, and SMTP delivery via Gmail OAuth.
  • Asset Agents Dashboard — phone-accessible private web artifact aggregating all agent states (flip regime, positions, IV scans, paper P&L, scheduled tasks) rebuilt weekday mornings; the only surface reachable on mobile when the Mac is unattended.
Jev Paper-Trading Desks
  • Jev platform — shared AI-judgement client (jev-client) used by every desk: audited quantized cache and daily spend governor, pause/flatten controls with an audit log, a unified ROI scoreboard measured against a 50–70%/yr minimum benchmark, and password-gated phone dashboards over Tailscale. Paper trading only; real money is gated behind written, measurable rules.
  • Supervisor & Guard — 30-second launchd supervisor restarts any dead agent and runs nightly backups; a stray-process guard kills runaway loops and alerts only on sustained CPU load or duplicate agents.
  • Jev HQ — control center for all desks: KPI strip, ROI table, system health, options flow and gamma panel, git-sync status for every repo, pause/resume/flatten/mute controls and a control log; includes this Asset Agents console as a tab.
  • Jev Options IV — paper options desk across 111 stocks in 11 GICS sectors plus 46 ETFs with six lanes (iron condor, condor max, put credit, call credit, bull call debit, earnings). Rules chosen profit-first from backtests with in-sample 2011–20 and out-of-sample 2021–26 splits on modelled option prices; each lane runs a $10k bank.
    • IV scorecard built from mid-price implied vol and verified against live Robinhood quotes, with an A/B gate comparing the code rule against a frozen Jev v1 read.
    • Free flow stack: unusual-volume sweep, 5-minute Cboe volume-diff flow tape with side inference, and gamma-level / 0DTE history with alerts.
  • Jev Markets — paper scalper and terminal for MNQ/MES with price-action entries and context from the options flow and 0DTE gamma regime; a mirrored invert arm runs the same logic on opposite trades in its own book as an A/B test.
  • Jev Majors & FOMO lane — paper perps desk over 26 coins priced on the Kalshi demo book with 1:1 fill parity, plus guarded and invert A/B lanes and a lane fed by sampled on-chain FOMO-app buys.
  • Jev Predict — paper desk on hourly BTC prediction-market contracts mirrored on the Kalshi demo book; GBM, stacked and pooled models were tested offline and rejected, and the results are documented. Live trading is gated on 80% win rate over at least 75 paper bets with positive net.
  • Jev Agents — plain-English prediction-market rule agents: a deterministic parser turns a sentence into a rule (odds, price, index move, gamma, flow triggers), a paper executor runs it, and a phone-installable app manages them.
  • Jev Shop, Shadow & AskJev — deal-finder UI with a designer-store lane; Chronos-Bolt and FinBERT shadow forecasts scored against a baseline with a written promotion rule; chat UI for questions to Jev.
