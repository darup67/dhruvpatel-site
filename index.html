About

Engineer first, embedded with the business.

I take a business problem, build the system that answers it, and prove it works before anyone relies on it.

I started in data engineering and moved steadily toward machine learning, then toward agents. At Sherwin-Williams I own the full lifecycle of the pricing-structure models. Before that I led a 25-person AI/ML organization at T-Mobile, and earlier built payments, fraud and compliance systems at Global Payments and Compliance.AI.

Outside work I build and run a lab of read-only agents that collect market data, detect signals and report by email. It exists to practice a discipline I care about: evaluation harnesses, guardrails, permission boundaries, kill switches and monitoring, written and debugged by hand.

Most of what it measures turns out to have no durable edge. That’s exactly what the harness is for.

Measure before you trust

Every output is graded against a baseline, net of costs. Only what clears the test is surfaced; the rest is logged and withheld.

Guardrails in code

Read-only scopes, confirm-before-action rules, credentials kept out of prompts and logs, and kill switches when live results decay.

Own the lifecycle

Feature engineering through training, validation, deployment and monitoring, then supporting adoption after release.

Projects

A lab built to tell me when I’m wrong.

Read-only agents that collect data, detect signals and report by email. None of them places orders. Each card says what the evidence actually showed.

Featured · Evaluation

trade-core & market-lab: an evidence layer for signals

A shared 15-minute market-data store and a signal ledger every agent writes to. Each signal is graded net of costs, tagged with its market regime and compared with random entry. A signal kind is only “proven” after 30+ graded signals over 10+ days with a Wilson lower bound above baseline; until then it stays out of alerts.

FoundAlmost nothing in the lab has a durable edge, and the harness is what says so.

Featured · Calibration

Kalshi callers: BTC (15-min and 1-hour), gold & WTI

Read-only agents that call Kalshi’s 15-minute up/down contracts only when the price cushion is large against the volatility still to come. The gate is set by a backtest rule fixed in advance and checked by a live scorecard. The gold and WTI version adapts the model to a single Pyth settlement candle and a proxy feed whose error is measured.

FoundAccurate calls, but Kalshi’s price already reflects them, so the edge is about zero after fees. Chronos and AutoGluon did not beat the market price at any minute of the 15-minute window or of the 1-hour (1,531 hours, 69 days).
